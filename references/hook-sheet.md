# Hook sheet

Line 1 of every script comes off this sheet. It is never invented fresh.

Source: the "Instant Hooks" sheet, card 2/6 (the original image is not bundled;
this page is the complete transcription).

Dashes have been rewritten out of every pattern below, because anything that
reaches a caption or an on screen title has to be clean of them.

Offer the person recording **three options, each a different pattern**, and name
which pattern each one is so they can see the framework.

## For this account specifically

The hook still has to carry **a named paid thing losing**, inside the first five
seconds. "DocuSign is $50 a month." "Higgsfield is $20 a month." A price the
viewer recognises, said out loud.

So pattern plus price, not pattern alone:

- 3 + price: "This is the truth no one tells you about the $50 a month one."
- 6 + price: "You're probably paying $20 a month for something you don't need."
- 17 + price: "The myth that you have to pay $50 a month to sign a document."

## Banned in a hook or a title

- the word **free** (say the licence: MIT, Apache 2.0, OSHWA, public domain)
- em dash, en dash, double hyphen
- the tool name, the repo, the URL
